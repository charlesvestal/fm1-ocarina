# OCA-1

OCA-1 turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into an ocarina for games that listen. It plays one clear note at a time, so the
Nintendo Switch 2 version of Ocarina of Time hears your songs through the console's microphone. The songbook has the
game's twelve songs. The keys you need light up in turn, PLAY plays a song for you, and OCA-1 names each song you
finish.

![OCA-1 halfway through Zelda's Lullaby: the songbook card with its six buttons, the five song notes and the four knobs](manual/img/screen-playing.png "OCA-1 on the FM-1's screen")

Version {{VERSION}}. Installing is at your own risk; the installer can put M-VAVE's own firmware back.

- [Try it in the browser](emu/): the same code the FM-1 runs, with sound. No FM-1 needed.
- [Install](install/): from Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 connected by USB.
- [Manual](manual/): playing a song to the game, the screen, the controls.
- [Download {{PKG}}](firmware/{{PKG}}): for the command-line installer, `python3 tools/fm1_install.py {{PKG}}`.
- [Source](https://github.com/charlesvestal/fm1-ocarina): MPL-2.0. Built on fm1-base and felucca-bsp, from Felucca
  by Leo Kuroshita, Hügelton Instruments.
