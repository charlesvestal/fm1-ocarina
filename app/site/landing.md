# OCA-1

OCA-1 turns your M-VAVE FM-1 into an ocarina for games that listen. It plays one clear note at a time, so the
Nintendo Switch 2 version of Ocarina of Time can hear your songs through the console's microphone. The songbook has
the game's twelve songs. The keys you need light up in turn, PLAY plays a song for you, and OCA-1 names each song
you finish.

![OCA-1 halfway through Zelda's Lullaby: the songbook card with its six buttons, the five song notes and the four knobs](manual/img/screen-playing.png "OCA-1 on the FM-1's screen")

This is version {{VERSION}}. Installing is at your own risk, and the installer can put M-VAVE's own firmware back.

- [Try it in the browser](emu/). Play it right here, with sound. It runs the same code as the FM-1, so you don't
  need one.
- [Install](install/). Connect your FM-1 by USB and install from Chrome or Edge.
- [Manual](manual/). Learn how to play a song to the game, read the screen and use the controls.
- [Download {{PKG}}](firmware/{{PKG}}). Use it with the command-line installer:
  `python3 base/tools/fm1_install.py {{PKG}}`.
- [Source](https://github.com/charlesvestal/fm1-ocarina). It's on GitHub under MPL-2.0. It's built on fm1-base and
  felucca-bsp, which grew out of Felucca by Leo Kuroshita (Hügelton Instruments).
